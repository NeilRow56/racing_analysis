# Phase 5 Hurdle and Chase course profitability development

**26 November 2026 · Research only · £1 win stakes**

## Executive summary

The 11 qualifying courses made **£179.61 from 869 settled T2 bets**. Among the five Hurdle courses, handicaps and longer-priced winners accounted for most of the gain. The six Chase courses had a broader spread of winners, with the largest gains in intermediate-distance races and Classes 4 and 5. Those conditions generally lost money at the remaining courses. Matching on handicap and distance left most of the historical difference intact. This audit cannot distinguish a persistent course effect from the favourable results used to select the courses.

**The strongest new hypothesis is to abstain in verified Class 4 Hurdles that the current short non-handicap rule would still bet.** It avoided £89.19 of T2 losses in 2025, £76.31 in earlier 2026 and £8.60 in the exploratory November frozen SP sample. On 42 November complete-board prices it avoided £4.50. The Class 4 trigger was weaker than the remaining eligible races in earlier 2026 and November, and stronger in 2025. That merits a bounded prospective test across all courses with an explicit comparison against skipping the same number of other races. The reconstructed whole-family policy still lost money at SP in both historical periods.

## Population and provenance

Of 14,373 wider inventory races, 627 had original frozen pre-off books. Hurdles and Chases account for 405 of those, including **32 at the selected courses** with **28 settled T2 stakes**. These ranks and prices were unknown before the race.
- **Genuine bookmaker evidence.** Quoted groups use complete verified boards. Earlier quoted samples have genuine prices but retrospective Tissue ranks. ## Development decision

**Advance only the Class 4 Hurdle rule to a future, bounded prospective comparison.** Freeze the exact rule above before collecting a fresh cohort. Apply it to all courses on a genuine pre-off book with a valid unchanged T2, pre-off card identity, numeric Class 4, confirmed handicap and yardage, and consistent frozen race inputs. Retain T2 when those fields are missing or inconsistent, subject to the established Hurdle policy in the incremental comparison. Record the decision timestamp, model identity, original T2 and bookmaker quote or explicit missing status, the existing policy decision and final settlement. Preserve quotes and refunds on both sides.

Use the first 150 settled trigger opportunities after the rule is frozen as an initial review, and report the parallel paired complete-board capture sample and results outside the five selected courses. Require the skipped bets to lose on both SP and captured-price bases, and compare them against an equal-stake control sample drawn from the current Hurdle shadow’s retained non-trigger opportunities within the same calendar months and handicap strata. Freeze control sampling before observing outcomes. Negative skipped P/L alone can result from broad T2 losses. Trigger P/L at or above zero, no worse ROI than the control, or an apparent gain driven by one venue that reverses elsewhere would count against the hypothesis. Use paired weekly or meeting summaries to assess sensitivity to clustered results. The sample target is a review point, with no guarantee of statistical precision.

This test asks whether Class 4 isolates losses among races that the existing distance and handicap rule still bets. The Chase Class 4–6 shadow continues to answer its separate rank-switch question. Phase 5 leaves the new rule as a research specification.
