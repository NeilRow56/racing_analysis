# Tissue 2 profitability by racecourse

**26 November 2026 · Research only · £1 win stakes**

**The best course-level research leads are Ffos Las Hurdles and the T2 versus T3 gap in Newcastle Chases.** Both patterns repeated historically, although Ffos Las went 0-for-8 in November and Newcastle has only two November SP pairs with T2 settled. Every All Weather course lost money in both historical periods. November's strongest AW results, at Lingfield and Southwell, reversed substantial historical losses.

### All Weather

- **Chelmsford City** had the best historical ROI and smallest net loss, **−£53.36 from 368 bets (−14.50%)**. Under 7f returned **+£20.13 from 123**, compared with **−£63.64 from 148** at 7–<10f. Its T2 beat T1 in both years on matched SP stakes, while T3 beat T2 in both. The combined matched T3 was **−£10.77** against **−£53.36** for T2 across **368** bets per rank.
- **Wolverhampton** had the largest loss, **−£254.50 from 1,145 (−22.23%)**. Its historical T2 trailed T1 in both years. The combined matched comparison was **−£251.50 versus −£158.82 across 1,142** per rank. T2's worst distance band was 7–<10f (**−£170.89 from 597**). The loss was almost exactly what other Tapeta cards of comparable handicap status and distance predicted, so gross loss alone adds little evidence of a Wolverhampton-specific effect.
- **Southwell** had the weakest combined ROI, **−25.88% on 912** (−£236.03), with **−£130.53 from 278** in fields of 11 or more. It still underperformed the other Tapeta comparisons. In November, **+£14.00 from 27 SP bets** became **−£2.00** without the largest winner. **Kempton** had the largest AW year-to-year ROI deterioration, from **−10.95% on 403** to **−25.47% on 292**. **Wolverhampton** improved from **−28.39% on 647** to **−14.22% on 498**.

## Five prospective research hypotheses

1. **Ffos Las Hurdles · Intermediate-distance handicaps.** T2 made **£9.40 from 55** and **£15.38 from 45**, and beat T1 and T3 separately in both years. The largest win contributed **£22.00** of £24.78 combined, leaving £2.78 after removal. Pooling the marginal groups, handicaps returned **+£40.54 on 62** and intermediate trips **+£40.04 on 53**. Test whether their intersection retains a T2 advantage against similarly conditioned Hurdles elsewhere. November's **−£8 on eight** at both bases argues for fresh evidence before using the pattern.
2. **Newcastle Chases · Existing Class 4–6 shadow as a diagnostic.** T2 lost **£19.50 on 30** and **£21.00 on 21**. Its largest winner paid **£4.00 net**. T3 made **+£26.96 and +£14.33** on the same annual races. T2 lost **£24.50 on 35 Class 4–6** selections and **£16.00 on 16 Class 1–3**, and the course remained weak after adjusting for handicap status, distance, class and market tier. Track Newcastle's contribution inside the existing Chase shadow and against comparable qualifying races elsewhere. The 51 historical stakes and two November SP bets do not justify a course gate.
3. **Kelso Chases · Intermediate handicaps and market support.** T2 made **£8.25 on 31** and **£22.42 on 26**, with a **£10.00** largest contribution. Removing each year's biggest winner leaves **−£0.25 and +£12.42**. Intermediate trips made **+£40.79 on 31**, and retrospective SP market ranks 4–6 made **+£35.50 on 22**. Compare existing T2 against T3 and matched peer handicaps on new intermediate cards, with pre-off quote rank fixed at capture. The November sample is **two stakes**, returning **+£1.50 at SP and +£1.25 captured**.
4. **Lingfield AW · Replicate the November reversal.** T2 lost **£98.87 on 326** and **£57.09 on 329**, and trailed T1 in both years. It then made **£19.21 at SP and £20.96 captured on 35** in November. The largest winning ticket contributed **£10 SP and £11 captured**, leaving both positive. Historically it lost **£144.92 on 477 handicaps** and **£131.96 on 429 retrospective market top-three selections**. Test whether the November gain recurs against T1 on new Lingfield cards and comparable Polytrack cards, with handicap status and genuine capture-time market tier recorded. This is a replication question.
5. **Punchestown Hurdles · Field-size and withdrawal diagnostic.** T2 lost **£33.12 from 92** and **£46.95 from 73**. The biggest historical win contributed **£18.00**, and fields of 11 or more lost **£68.94 on 98**. November made **£10 SP and £13 captured on 14**, but the largest winning ticket contributed **£14 SP and £16 captured**. On future large-field cards, compare T2 with matched Hurdles elsewhere and report withdrawal-free results separately. The goal is to tell a field-composition effect from a few high-priced winners.

## Settlement and verification

Historical returns come from the existing proved full-result records using exact fractional SP converted to decimal. Winners count winning tickets, with fractional win credit provided separately. Dead heats divide the entire gross return by the official divisor. Non-runners and voids carry zero stake. Captured returns are nominal median bookmaker quotes before any bookmaker-specific deductions. The largest-winner sensitivity omits one winning ticket and its stake. The largest and top-two gross share columns help identify concentration. Each T1 or T3 comparison uses its own identical eligible pair with frozen T2, so its T2 total can differ from the headline T2 figure.
