# AW V3 original T2 selection diagnostic

**Field-relative Speed and recent performance were the clearest repeatable form signals for winning. Verified pre-off market rank separated winners and losers more strongly than any of the form measures. Neither finding established a repeatable historical profit across both years.**

## Populations and method

- **A25** is the 2025 broad historical reconstruction. **A26** is the earlier 2026 reconstruction through 21 September. These populations use the original relative AW V3 scores from saved historical starter fields. Target declaration timing is unverified and 2025 overlaps the AW V2 base fit.
- **B26** contains races anchored to verified pre-off data from 28 September through 20 November 2026. It comprises 132 archived-card reconstructions, 150 frozen AW V2 fields scored with the AW V3 formula in the prior study, and 121 original AW V3 captures. The earlier study used a prior-day cutoff for historical features in the archived-card segment. These 403 races include 79 races from AW V3 adjustment development. There is no equivalent 2025 B cohort.
- Each selection is the original AW V3 T2 from the existing matched population. The earlier study's price-complete and starter-match rules still define the denominator, including its exclusions due to withdrawals among compared picks. This diagnostic reads the saved original T2 IDs and joins the corresponding frozen or archived input vector. It makes no new predictions. Exact runner IDs, final SP, winner credit and canonical £1 P/L reconciled to the retained database snapshot. All race counts below are distinct and use one £1 win stake per race. [Prior population and component inventory](aw-v3-same-or-component-research.md) · [Settlement and source audit](full-history-profitability-model-research.md#1-database-and-source-coverage)
- The bands were fixed before examining their grouped returns. Field ranks use competition ranks over available values. Speed trend is latest AW Speed minus the available last-three mean, which already includes the latest figure. Class movement, last actual finish and trip evidence use the horse's strictly earlier observed AW runs in the audited dataset, seeded by 2024. A similar trip is within 10% of target distance among the last eight observed AW runs. The four counted missing inputs are OR, Best L3 AW Speed, latest AW performance and trainer prior win rate. The trainer measure is the original frozen percentage from earlier starts.
- Verified market position ranks a complete, timestamped field of median bookmaker quotes. It was available for 380 of 403 B26 races and for 41 A26 races with a separately archived complete quote board that matched the historical starter field. The 41 remain in A26 because their model inputs come from the historical reconstruction. No A25 quote board qualified. Final SP is a settlement price. Its price bands are retrospective descriptions.

## Information the frozen score does not represent directly

- **Verified pre-off market position.** AW V3 scores the field without bookmaker prices. The archived market rank was the strongest observed separator where a full board existed. The 89 B26 favourites are still a small, late-2026 subset. Their positive return was absent from one of the three B26 sources. The available quotes are bookmaker medians and do not establish execution at a particular bookmaker.
- **Explicit prior finish and trip record.** AW V3 carries rated performances and prior comments, which can already reflect finishing performance. It has no direct last-AW-top-three indicator or runner-specific history at this trip. Target distance is shared within a race and surface terms concern material. Past top-three finishes separated winners in all populations. The similar-trip result reversed in B26, giving weaker evidence for distance matching.
- **Comparable Speed history.** The latest-minus-mean proxy shares the latest observation with its reference average. It cannot distinguish a one-figure history from a sustained trend at comparable trips and surfaces. An explicit chronological sequence on a comparable basis would resolve that ambiguity. The observed proxy has no stable directional association.

The results describe a selected T2 population. They do not identify causal feature effects or an independently profitable new selection rule. No model, racecard process, settlement record or frozen input changed.
