# Tissue profitability decision review — 12 November 2026

**There is insufficient evidence that either component change moves us towards repeatable positive ROI. Both warrant continued examination through existing frozen infrastructure only: decision A. Neither warrants new monitoring fields, a new monitor, a production change or a betting-rule change.**

There are positive realised research cohorts, including older Chase Shadow T1 and some T2 cohorts. They do not establish that either specific component causes an incremental profit advantage. The approximate 5% long-term ROI target was not used to select a strategy, price cut, component weight or favourable sample.

## The exact two hypotheses

From the [component-attribution report](tissue-component-attribution-2026-11-12.md), conclusions F:

1. **AW same-OR context:** “assess whether the negative same-OR contribution displaces winners relative to frozen V2, especially when it changes Top2 membership.” Family AW; `AW_TISSUE_V2`→`AW_TISSUE_V3`. The V3 same-OR Speed-gap/10 term has effective slope −.333090. It was part of the small regularised context addition selected on later-block log loss; no separate component-profitability rationale was established.
2. **Chase comment-coefficient changes in old-leader demotions:** “test whether comment-margin changes identify harmful old-T1→new-T2 moves beyond the numeric block, comparing original/full/new-comments-only and old-comments-restored diagnostic scores.” Family Chase; shared `JUMP_TISSUE_V1`→`CHASE_SHADOW_V1`→production `CHASE_TISSUE_V2`. The joint Chase-only refit changed 42/43 effective comment slopes, alongside numeric coefficients. Original rationale was Chase-specific probability quality; it was not a separately validated increase in comment profitability.

These are the only hypotheses reviewed. Positive/Neutral, Value captures and joint context-shadow results below are existing evidence streams, not additional hypotheses or proposed strategies.

Evidence for investigation is narrower than evidence of profit. The prior AW term-alone historical reconstruction worsened loss and Top1/Top2 capture, but had no captured-price portfolio proving harm. Chase's original larger joint-refit reference improved probability quality, while exact recent attribution and winning leader demotions raised a contrary ordering concern. Neither establishes a comment-specific profit effect. The financial evidence below therefore takes priority over those ranking findings.

## Financial basis and cohort boundaries

All amounts below are **new read-only canonical Settlement V3 calculations**, staking £1 per eligible priced selection, on frozen raw result evidence and original bookmaker quotes. Old stored cash totals are not reused or relabelled. Non-runners/voids have no stake; recognised non-finishers lose; unknown/invalid outcomes remain unresolved; full decimal returns are divided in dead heats. No odds cap, SP substitution, inferred quote, new result extraction or database refresh was used. Captured quotes are research prices, not proof that bets were executed.

Unlike the earlier attribution report's strict whole-field quality population, this financial review retains a valid selected starter even if another horse in its captured field later became NR. Original T1/T2 selections are never replaced. Thus AW V3 T1 has 45 priced starters here, versus 34 in the earlier NR-free comparison. The earlier +£2.35/+6.91% is a narrower population result; this broader eligible monetary cohort is −£8.65/−19.22%. This is a disclosed denominator change, not a rewritten outcome. It avoids discarding legitimate returns merely because other runners withdrew.

Market expectation uses inverse **captured full-field** prices normalized to one at capture, without post-withdrawal renormalisation. A/E numerator uses fractional winner credit on precisely that complete-market priced subset. The existing context-shadow report uses a raw inverse-price A/E convention; the normalized version here is explicitly different. Missing full books cannot supply A/E; a valid selected quote may still supply a cash result. Winners in the table are all settled selections; the separate “market N / credit” column exposes the narrower A/E numerator/denominator. Unpriced settled selections never become stakes or zero P/L.

Source outcomes include unversioned, recorded V2 and recorded V3 entries. **Every displayed monetary calculation is V3**, grouped by model/cohort/collection epoch; the appendix separately partitions original recorded bases and excludes unresolved records. No old-basis cash result is combined with V3 cash. Race-stage evidence absent from an older snapshot is not newly certified. All streams can overlap in races and may have different cutoffs; none is pooled into an overall ROI.

## Evidence required before reconsideration

Retain the two definitions and current frozen models. Use **later observations not included in this review's snapshot**, through the existing authorised streams, at predeclared calendar review points rather than checking until a favourable ROI appears. Keep old/new epochs, T1/T2, true shadows and counterfactuals separately labelled. No further prospective information was found essential to calculate these two component margins: full vectors, cutoffs, model identities, field prices and result evidence already exist. Missing prices or missed shadow pairs must remain missing.

The primary question is a positive **paired net cash-return difference**, alongside positive standalone returns at valid captured prices, with uncertainty sufficiently narrow to distinguish a useful advantage from zero. Examine chronological stability and the existing price-band/large-winner sensitivities; do not demand that every band win or discard legitimate large returns. Capture coverage and an agreed uncertainty method must be specified before examining the later outcomes. Race-day clustering or another dependence-aware interval is preferable to the illustrative simple race intervals here. Repeated exploratory looks and an insufficiently priced changed leader cannot count as confirmation.

The 5% target is an economic aim, not a historically selected hurdle or reason to stop at +5%. A future point estimate near 5% with an interval spanning substantial losses would remain inconclusive. No arbitrary minimum winner count or new ROI threshold is proposed. The context monitor's existing 300-race sample label is not a guarantee of profitable evidence.

For AW, reconsider only if the predeclared single-term financial comparison and the genuinely frozen joint-shadow evidence clarify, rather than contradict, each other on later data. For Chase, establish whether the frozen comment-margin change explains cash effects beyond numeric changes, with both T1 and rank-2 consequences retained. Neither better winner capture nor lower loss/calibration error substitutes for positive net returns.

**Conclusion:** passive continuation of existing monitoring is justified as evidence collection; belief in a repeatable component-driven positive ROI is not currently justified. No extra monitor, component field, new epoch or production change is recommended or activated.
